# Events:  
Events in google analytics are user interactions on your website that can not be tracked as page views or screen views within the session. 
Any activity that user stays on the page is an event or event click.  
**Eg:** Button clicks, Clicks or outbound links, Form submissions, Video plays, Downloads.  
These are the way to know if the users are interacting with your website and performing intended actions.  

-- Google analytics tracks the total events which are the total number of interactions with the track element. Unique event is a metric to 
learn how many users triggered the event.  

## Unique visitors:  
How many users visited the website. Unique visitors are users and Visits are labelled as sessions.  

## Sessions:  
In marketing analytics, a session is a group of user interactions that take place on your website or application within a given timeframe.  

## Organic search or Paid search:  
## ORG:  
These results are unpaid listings that appear naturally on search engines based on relevance.  
**Key features:**  
-- no payment required  
-- controlled by Google's algorithm  
-- Based on SEO(Search Engine Optimization)  
-- Takes time to rank  

**How it works:**  
Google ranks pages based on:  
-- content relevation  
-- Keywords used  
-- Website quality  
-- Backlinks(popularity)  
-- Authority(trust level)  
-- User experience(speed, mobile friendly)  

## Paid search:  
These are advertisements where companies pay to appear in search results.  

**Key features:**  
-- Paid (PPC-Paid per click)  
-- Appears with "Ad" or "Sponsored" label  
-- Immediate results  
-- Fully controlled by the company  

**what companies control:**  
- Keywords
- Ad design(text)
- Budge
- Target Audience

**Important notes:**  
Companies don't create organic results. They use content to search engine optimization. Companies create PS results by paying for them.
